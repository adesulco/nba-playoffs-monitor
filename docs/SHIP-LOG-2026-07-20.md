## R1 — Sistem 4a foundation · COMPLETE (v0.82.0)

**Shipped.** The locked redesign's substrate, on the untouched engine — nothing user-visible flips yet. Fonts are self-hosted latin subsets at **50KB against an 80KB budget**: pinning Bricolage Grotesque to weight 800 (the only weight the design uses) cut it from 75KB to 21KB versus the full variable file, and Instrument Sans stays variable 400–700 because that beats shipping four static instances. `tokens-4a.css` carries the full palette, type, shape and spacing tables plus the Edisi Malam dark set, with **legacy names aliased** to 4a values so unported surfaces render in the new palette rather than drifting half-old. The six primitives (MatchCard, PickChip with its five exact states, LeaderboardRow, LiveTile, KabarCard, LockBadge) are presentation-only — they take data and callbacks and never fetch, so R2 composes them against the `src/pickem/api.js` seam. Plus `sportSkins.js` (keyed by sport, never by competition), an inline-SVG icon set with no icon dependency, the pure-CSS GI/BOL logo block, and the theme engine.

**Two decisions worth recording.** Edisi Malam computes its 19:00–06:00 window from a **fixed UTC+7** rather than the device clock: WIB never observes DST, and "Malam Ini" has to mean the same instant for a whole grup whoever's phone is where. The manual override is sticky-but-self-clearing — it remembers which side of the schedule you chose and expires once the schedule catches up, so forcing dark at noon doesn't strand you in dark forever. Second, `check-vocab.mjs` gained a **register guard** enforcing kamu/-mu and rejecting lo/gue, matching only inside string literals and JSX text because "lo" and "gue" collide with real tokens (lock, logo, league). It found 8 pre-existing violations on its first run, all migrated.

**Verified in a real browser at 390×844**, not just built: `/dev/primitives` renders all six primitives across three sport skins in both themes, and both new fonts report `loaded`. The QA route immediately paid for itself by catching a bug that a build could never surface — the dark tokens were scoped to `:root[data-theme4a]` only, so the Edisi Malam section rendered light. Dark tokens now also match a bare `[data-theme4a]`, which R2's always-dark share cards need anyway.
